# Milestone 1 Notes

## Domain snapshot

The guide focuses on Georgia Tech OMSCS course-selection and workload advice, especially the gap between official course descriptions and the student-reported reality of difficulty, time cost, and course fit.

## Source structure observations

- Official OMSCS course pages are short, highly structured, and predictable: overview, learning goals, suggested background, and administrative details.
- OMSCentral pages are much noisier. They mix many short review entries, repeated rating/workload metadata, and free-text comments that often hide the most useful details in one or two sentences.
- The Reddit megathread is conversational and cross-course. It is useful for sequencing and comparison questions, but it will likely need smaller chunks than the official pages because one thread can jump across several topics quickly.

## Questions this corpus should answer

1. What background does CS 6515 expect before a student enrolls?
2. Why do students find CS 7641 difficult, beyond just "it is hard"?
3. Is CS 6300 a reasonable first OMSCS course for someone newer to software engineering?
4. What do students warn each other about in CS 6310 group projects?
5. Is CS 6603 usually treated as a lighter course, and what tradeoff do students mention when they say that?
